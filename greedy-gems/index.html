A Roblox Game

Greedy Gems

In development, not yet public on Roblox.

A Roblox mining game I am building with a small team. Gems float down the river with a price tag: buy a shard, set it in your socket, and cash out while its value climbs, before the cave in. Only a gem you cash out in time can be set in your claim, where it mines the chips that pay you while you play.

My role: everything, from the game design and the Luau scripting to the in game economy and the art.

1

The Loop

The gamble is the only way to supply the mine. You cannot build a claim without winning bets.

  1. Buy a shard. A new gem floats down the river every five seconds, each with a price tag.
  2. Sink it. Set it in the crash socket on your plot. The multiplier climbs from zero, and the crystal grows with it.
  3. Cash out, or shatter. Cash out in time and you keep a crystallized gem worth its price times the multiplier. Wait too long and the cave in shatters it, leaving 40% of its value.
  4. Set it. Only a crystallized gem can be set in your claim. Every gem standing there is a bet you won.
  5. Mine it. Set gems shed chips you walk over to collect, the passive income. Four chips make a ticket, and tickets buy pet eggs.
2

The Economy

The numbers are built so the gamble feels risky without ever wiping a player out, and so the long game rewards time, not luck.

  1. A floor that cannot lose. A bare shard's cave in never comes before 2.50x, and a crash keeps 40% of the value: 2.50 × 0.40 = 1.00. The worst possible run returns exactly what the shard cost, so greed decides how much more you make, never whether you get wiped out.
  2. Patience pays. Cash out a fully grown gem, at 7x or better, and it pays 1.4 times its value.
  3. Two currencies, kept apart. Coins come from the gamble. Tickets come only from mining, and only tickets buy pet eggs, so a lucky run cannot buy the best pet.
  4. Rebirth is the long game. Six rebirths, from $500 up to 100 quintillion coins. Each one resets your coins but keeps your pets, tickets and claim, grows the claim, and opens something new: the Crusher, the Adoption Center, Wheel Spin, Furniture.
  5. Rarity you can see before you can afford it. 28 gems across twelve tiers, Common to Godly. Watching a rare one drift past long before you can buy it is what keeps a session going.

The numbers are still being tuned before launch.

3

The Systems Behind It

The game runs on 142 Luau scripts, about 88,000 lines, on Roblox's servers and each player's device.

  • The riverA new gem every five seconds, priced and sold as a shard, with rare ones promised on a timer.
  • The crash socketRolls each run's cave in point, then pays the cash out or the 40% keep.
  • Claims and chipsGrows set gems into clusters that shed chips, the passive income.
  • Pets and eggsTicket priced eggs, a hatch scene, and pets that boost your mine.
  • RebirthSix levels that grow your claim and open new parts of the game.
  • The indexA collection book of every gem and mutation you have found.
  • Wheel Spin and leaderboardsA prize wheel that rebirth opens, and boards for the top players.
  • SavesEvery player's claim and progress, kept between visits.
