What the Numbers Say

  1. Program ALX-7 finished December at CPI 0.93 and SPI 0.91, with cost variance (1,800) and schedule variance (2,100).

A TCPI of 1.076 means the remaining work must earn about 1.08 of value per dollar spent, about 7.6% better than break even, against the 0.93 CPI achieved to date, to recover to plan.

Model Checks

Each check is a formula on the workbook's Checks sheet. All of them pass.

  • Balance sheet balances, every year
  • Cash flow ending cash equals balance sheet cash, every year
  • Net income ties, income statement to cash flow, every year
  • Beginning cash equals prior year ending cash, every year
  • Retained earnings rolls forward, every year
  • Total revenue equals its components, every year
  • Total current assets equal their components, every year
  • Total liabilities equal their components, every year
