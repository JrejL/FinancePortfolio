Model checks

Each check is a formula on the workbook's Checks sheet. All of them pass.

  • The balance sheet balances at the opening and at every month end
  • Cash flow ending cash equals balance sheet cash at December 31
  • Cash flow opening cash equals the opening balance sheet
  • Income statement net income equals the sum of the twelve months
  • Cash flow statement starts from the same net income
  • Cash flow statement ending cash equals the balance sheet
  • Equity statement ending balance equals balance sheet equity
  • Balance sheet statement: total assets equal the monthly model
  • Balance sheet statement balances
  • Merchandise sales equal the SQL control total
  • Sales by channel equal sales by product line
  • Orders equal the SQL control total
  • Budget shares add up to 100%
  • Cash is never negative
  • Inventory is never negative
