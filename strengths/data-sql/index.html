What the Numbers Say

  1. eBay brought 58% of sales on fewer orders. Its average order was $111.57 against $49.46 on Shopify.
  2. The gap is product mix: 65% of designer fragrance sales came through eBay, while 81% of Car Perfume and refill sales, the lower priced items, came through Shopify.
