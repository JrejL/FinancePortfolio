Accounting

Closing the books: journal entries, reconciliations and accruals, then an adjusted trial balance that ties to the statements.

  • Month End Close
  • Journal Entries
  • Account Reconciliations
  • Accruals and Prepaids
  • Financial Statements and Notes
  • Fund Accounting
1

How I Use It

  1. Every journal entry balances, and every reconciliation ends at a difference of zero.
  2. Accruals land in the right period and reverse in the next one.
  3. The adjusted trial balance becomes the statements with no manual step in between.
2
