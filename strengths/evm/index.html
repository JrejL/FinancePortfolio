Earned Value Management (EVM)

Measuring a program's cost and schedule performance the way defense programs report it: CPI, SPI, EAC and TCPI.

  • CPI and SPI
  • Estimate at Completion
  • TCPI
  • Variance at Completion
  • Contract Economics
  • Backlog and Book to Bill
1

How I Use It

  1. Cost and schedule efficiency read from planned value, earned value and actual cost every month.
  2. The estimate at completion forecast more than one way, with a view on which one to trust.
  3. Program performance tied to the contract's fee and the company's margin.

What the Numbers Say

  1. Program ALX-7 finished December at CPI 0.93 and SPI 0.91, with cost variance (1,800) and schedule variance (2,100).
  2. The estimate at completion ranges from 51,840 using CPI to 54,410 using CPI and SPI, both above the 48,000 budget at completion. A TCPI of 1.076 means the remaining work must earn about 1.08 of value per dollar spent, about 7.6% better than break even, against the 0.93 CPI achieved to date, to recover to plan.

Earned Value Forecast, ALX-7 Program

Given the cost and schedule performance on ALX-7 through December 2025, where does the program end up?

Computes cumulative CPI and SPI by month for Aldercrest's ALX-7 program and three estimate at completion methods from the December figures.

Estimate at Completion, December 2025

MethodEACVAC
BAC / CPI51,840(3,840)

VAC is BAC minus EAC; a negative VAC is a projected overrun against the 48,000 budget at completion.

MetricValue

What the Numbers Say

  1. CPI ends the year at 0.93 and SPI at 0.91, so ALX-7 is both over cost and behind schedule against its cumulative plan.
  2. The three EAC methods range from 49,800 to 54,410 against a budget at completion of 48,000, all pointing to an overrun.
  3. To complete performance index to BAC is 1.08, above 1.0, meaning the remaining work needs to run more efficiently than the program has run so far just to hit the original budget.
  4. CPI falls every month through December to 0.93, while SPI bottoms in Oct at 0.910 before recovering to 0.915 by December.
