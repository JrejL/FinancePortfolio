What the Numbers Say

  1. Restaurant level margin fell from 20.0% in FY2023 to 17.7% in FY2024 as labor rose to 32.6% of sales: California's $20 minimum wage for fast food chains with more than 60 locations took effect in April 2024, and Sunfield, below that threshold, raised pay to keep its crews. Margin recovered part of that to 18.6% in FY2025 as menu pricing caught up.
  2. FY2025 sales missed budget by (1,630) (67,270 actual against 68,900 budget) as two openings slipped a quarter on permits and Q4 traffic ran softer. The dollar variances on labor and food read favorable only because spending fell along with the sales miss: as a share of sales, labor ran 32.9% against a 32.4% budget and food ran 29.8% against a 29.5% budget, both over rate as new restaurants ramped and food cost saw pressure from eggs and avocados.
