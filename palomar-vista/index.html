Case study 05 of 06, local government

City of Palomar Vista, California

Fictional company. All figures are illustrative and were built for this portfolio.

Palomar Vista is a Southern California city of 84,000. This case study links the General Fund's budget to actual results, its declining reserve ratio and a five year forecast that shows the structural gap widening without action. Governmental funds use modified accrual accounting and report no cash flow statement.

Model checks

Each check is a formula on the workbook's Checks sheet. All of them pass.

  • Balance sheet fund balance equals Fund Activity ending fund balance, every year
  • Fund balance beginning equals prior year ending, every year
  • Total revenues equal their components, every year
  • Total expenditures equal their components, every year
  • Cash and investments is positive, every year
