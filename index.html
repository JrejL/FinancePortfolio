02

Variance report

Takes a budget and the actuals, computes every variance, and flags only the lines that matter: more than 5% or more than $250K off. The flags go straight into an Excel summary.

In progress
# Flag what a manager should look at first
bva["variance"] = bva["actual"] - bva["budget"]
bva["variance_pct"] = bva["variance"] / bva["budget"]
big = (bva.variance_pct.abs() > 0.05) | (bva.variance.abs() > 250)
bva[big].to_excel("variance_flags.xlsx", index=False)
03

Cash forecast simulation

Runs the 13 week cash forecast ten thousand times with collections that vary week to week, then reports how often cash would dip below the minimum.

In progress
# 10,000 versions of the next 13 weeks
rng = np.random.default_rng(7)
shock = rng.normal(1.0, 0.08, size=(10_000, 13))
flows = receipts * shock - payments
ending = start + flows.cumsum(axis=1)
(ending.min(axis=1) < 1_500).mean()

04

How I work

The same five steps behind every case study here, and behind the work I would do for you.

  1. Step 1Pull the dataTransactions into a database, shaped with SQL and checked against the source.
  2. Step 2Build the modelThree linked statements in Excel, with every input in one place.
  3. Step 3Make it tieChecks on every statement, so an error shows up before anyone reads the report.
  4. Step 4Find the storyVariances, trends and ratios that explain what changed and why.
  5. Step 5Present itA short memo, a dashboard or a deck that someone outside finance can follow.
