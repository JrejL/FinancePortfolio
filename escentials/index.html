My Business

Escentials

Escentials is my e-commerce business. It runs two lines on one Shopify store: designer fragrance from 446 brands, shipped straight from the supplier, and Car Perfume, a small glass bottle of its own that hangs from a rearview mirror. I set the catalog and the prices, keep the books, and direct the website.

What I Do

  1. Pricing. I set a tiered markup on supplier cost, capped at the supplier's retail price, found 55 variants selling below cost, and repriced them.
  2. The catalog. 5,678 active products from 446 brands, sorted into categories and brand pages, with stock synced from the supplier.
  3. Car Perfume. The store's own line: 14 scents in ten bottle colors, with refills in 10, 20 and 30 ml and a deal on each.
  4. The website. I direct the rebuild: a category wheel, a Brands page that builds in about 0.16 seconds instead of up to 2.9 (in the next release), and the live 3D Car Perfume above.
  5. The books. FY2025 statements, budget versus actual, a SQL customer database and a Tableau dashboard, on their own page.
