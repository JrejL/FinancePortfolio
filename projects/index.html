    Greedy Gems

    A Roblox mining game I am building with a small team.

    • Game Design
    • Luau
    • Economy
    See the project →
    03 A Roblox Game

    Gems float down a river with a price tag. Buy one, sink it in your socket and cash out while its value climbs, before the cave in. Only a gem you cash out in time can be set in your claim, where it mines the chips that pay you while you play.

    My part
    Everything: the game design, the Luau scripting, the in game economy and the art.
    Tools and skills
    • Roblox Studio
    • Luau
    • Game Economy
    • Probability
    • UI Design
    Results
    • 28 gems to collect, across twelve rarity tiers
    • 142 Luau scripts, about 88,000 lines of code
    • 2.50 × 0.40 = 1 the rule that means a bare shard can never lose money
    • 10 mutations, each multiplying a gem's value, up to 100x
    Timeline
    Started in September 2026 with a small team.
    Status
    In development, not yet public on Roblox.

  4. Everything Bible

    An iPhone app for tracking what you have read in the Bible, with notes.

    • iOS
    • SwiftUI
    • Product Design
    See the project →
    04 An iOS App

    Tap a chapter to mark it read and watch all 66 books fill in over the years. The full King James text is built in and works offline, with notes on any chapter or verse, a Verse of the Day and a Home Screen widget.

    My part
    I designed the app and directed the build, screen by screen: the features, the layout, and rules like milestones instead of streaks.
    Tools and skills
    • Swift
    • SwiftUI
    • SwiftData
    • WidgetKit
    • Xcode
    Results
    • 31,102 verses built in, checked against the canon, readable offline
    • 1,189 chapters to track, by book, testament and section
    • 2 languages: English and Spanish
    • 37 Swift files, about 4,500 lines of code
    Timeline
    Idea on September 10, 2026. Built on September 30, 2026.
    Status
    Running on the iPhone simulator. Not yet on the App Store.
